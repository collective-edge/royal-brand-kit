How booking works

Royal Ambulance

2

A ride booked in the morning is a ride that arrives on time.

Tested at two hospitals from 1 October to 11 November.

3

Booking takes four steps.

  • Send the request through the ordering page.
  • Royal replies with a unit and a pickup window.
  • The crew calls the nursing station before arrival.
  • Call dispatch if the discharge moves.

4

Book by 10:00. Royal confirms in 15 minutes.

  • Request through the ordering page.
  • Dispatch replies with a pickup window.
  • The crew calls 20 minutes before arrival.

Request by 10:00 for these windows

5

On-time pickup rose at every level of service.

  • Basic life support gained the most.
  • Critical care was already booked by phone.
  • No level got worse.

7

A morning request gets a window.

Before 10:00

  • Confirmed within 15 minutes.
  • A named unit and a pickup window.
  • Planned with the morning list.
  • The crew calls ahead.

After 10:00

  • Confirmed after the morning list.
  • The earliest open window.
  • May move to the next morning.
  • The crew calls ahead.

8

A booked ride follows the same four steps.

Before 10:00

Request

The case manager books on the ordering page.

Within 15 minutes

Confirmation

Dispatch names the unit and the window.

20 minutes out

Call ahead

The crew calls the nursing station.

In the window

Pickup

The unit holds 15 minutes past the window.
