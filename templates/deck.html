Royal Ambulance · Interfacility transport 02

Q3 · nine partners

Bed to wheels is the number partners feel.

The clock runs from discharge order release to departure at the origin door. It is the only interval a charge nurse watches, and it is the one Royal controls end to end. The median held at 38.6 minutes across nine partners in Q3.

The slowest quartile sat at 71 minutes. Every case in it traced to paperwork not ready on the floor, no gurney inside the county, or a receiving bed nobody had confirmed. Two of the three are fixed at the origin, not in the rig.
