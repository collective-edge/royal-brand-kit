1.2

Space and size

Keep x clear on all four sides. Nothing enters it: no type, no rule, no edge. Minimums are screen width in pixels and print width in inches.

  1. R01The fill on light is #43205B in every lockup. The fill on dark is #FFFFFF.
  2. R02On a header band the logo is 1.6in wide.
  3. R03If the clear space is short, shrink the logo.
  4. R04Under 1.5in wide, use the horizontal lockup in place of the stacked one.

2.3

Alerts

Four meanings, one family. Green means good and nothing else. A brand color is never a status. The word always travels with the color.

2.4

Charts

Chart color is a separate house set of eight. A brand color is never a series color. Assign in order. Never cycle. In a scatter, four series at most on light and three on dark. Darker means more, so the ramp can carry the brand.

03

Type

Montserrat for every word. Weights 400, 600, 700 and 800. No italic, ever.

3.1

Family

One typeface for every word in the house. Four weights and nothing else. Three at most in one piece. 800 is for hero and title bands only. The kit also defines a mono face, .bk-code, for hex values, paths and code. This page marks those strings with the class and sets them in Montserrat.

3.2

Scale

Twelve steps. Three sizes above body at most in one piece. This page sets its text at 12, 14, 16, 20 and 40.

3.3

Setting and space

Ten spacing steps on a base of 4, and the six rules that decide most pages.

  1. R05Uppercase is always tracked and never longer than four words.
  2. R06Flush left, ragged right. Never justify. Never center past three lines.
  3. R07On a dark ground, add 0.005em tracking and drop one weight at 700 or heavier. 600 is the floor.
  4. R08Space above a heading is three times the space below it. 48 above, 16 below.
  5. R09A body line stops at 70 characters, 54ch. Captions 40ch. Headings 28ch.
  6. R10Corners are square. Diagram nodes take 6px. Shadow signals elevation only.

05

Rules

The short list. Use, avoid, and how the brand writes.

5.1

Use and avoid

Every rule on this page carries one number, R01 onward. Cite the number.

Use

  1. R11Open every document on the Dark Purple #2F193B band.
  2. R12Use Purple #572E72 for accents and brand-colored text on white.
  3. R13Set body text in #23232A.
  4. R14Keep white text on Dark Purple, Purple or Royal Purple only.
  5. R15Use the horizontal lockup wherever it fits.
  6. R16Add Powered by Collective Edge once, beside the Royal mark.

Avoid

  1. R17Do not set Light Purple #8260A2 as body text.
  2. R18Do not use Light Purple as a large background.
  3. R19Do not put #8260A2 on #2F193B.
  4. R20Do not use a brand purple as a status color.
  5. R21Do not recolor a logo or invert it with a CSS filter.
  6. R22Do not separate the crown from the wordmark in the horizontal lockup.
  7. R23Do not use a background tint stronger than #F0E1FD.
  8. R24Do not sample a partner facility color from a logo.

5.2

Voice

Written for the reader who is between calls. Short declarative sentences.

  1. R25Write for the reader who is between calls.
  2. R26Short declarative sentences, active verbs.
  3. R27Name partners and facilities exactly as they write themselves.
  4. R28No em dashes. A period, a comma or a middot.

Where it goes

  1. R29The footer of a site
  2. R30The sidebar header of any dashboard
  3. R31The footer row of the closing slide of a deck, small
  4. R32The footer row of a memo or a one-pager, on page 1, small
  5. R33The last line of a report body, small
  6. R34A data page or a methodology page
  7. R35Anywhere a number was computed rather than typed

Do not

  1. R36Put it on the partner’s own mark or inside their lockup
  2. R37Merge, nest or recolor either mark to match the other
  3. R38Put it on a clinical instruction
  4. R39Put it on a patient-facing consent or safety document
  5. R40Use it twice in one document or on one surface
  6. R41Set the phrase as type where the mark fits. A chart or data graphic made on its own is the one place the words stand alone
  7. R42Design the lockup down to the 120px floor on a site or a dashboard
  8. R43Set it larger than 1.25in in a PDF, a deck or an image
  9. R44Put it on a working document or a sheet, as a lockup or as words
