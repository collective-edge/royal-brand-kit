Two traps, both measured

  • Light Purple #8260a2 is never body text and never a large background. It is a tertiary accent and a hover state.
  • Never put Light Purple on Dark Purple. It measures 3.13:1 and fails at every size. On a dark ground use #E0D6E8 for body and #B0A2BC for captions.
  • White text belongs only on Dark Purple, Purple or Royal Purple. Never white on Light Purple.

Type

Do not

  • Stretch, squash, skew or rotate the logo.
  • Recolor it. Pick the file that matches the background.
  • Add a drop shadow, glow, outline or gradient.
  • Separate the crown from the wordmark in the horizontal lockup.
  • Place it on a busy photograph without a solid backing panel.
  • Render it below the minimum sizes above.
