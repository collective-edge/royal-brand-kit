# Royal Ambulance · names and logos

The names, logos and brand colors in this repository belong to Royal Ambulance. That covers the Royal Ambulance name, the horizontal and stacked lockups and the crown.

This repository is public so that the people who make Royal Ambulance material can reach the files. Public does not mean free to use. The files are here to be used for Royal Ambulance, with its knowledge.

## You may, without asking

- Use the logos on work that Royal Ambulance asked you to make: printing, signage, a website, a document, a deck.
- Show the logo, unaltered, to name Royal Ambulance as a partner, customer, employer or vendor, where that is true and Royal Ambulance knows about it.
- Use the name in plain text to refer to the company: news, reviews, comparisons, job histories.
- Read the standard and learn from it: the layout rules, the type system and the checks.

## Ask first

- Any use of a logo on something you sell, or in advertising.
- Any use that suggests Royal Ambulance sponsors, endorses or is part of something it did not agree to.
- A co-branded piece that Royal Ambulance has not seen.
- Any use on vehicles, uniforms, patches or badges.

## Never

- Redraw, recolor, stretch, crop or add to a logo. Use the files as they ship.
- Use the name or a logo in a company name, product name, domain or social handle.
- Use the name, a logo or the palette in a way that could pass your work off as coming from Royal Ambulance.
- Present yourself as Royal Ambulance or as its staff.

## How to ask

Open an issue in this repository, or ask your contact at Royal Ambulance. Say what you want to make, where it will appear and for how long. Permission is given case by case and in writing. No answer is not a yes.

Royal Ambulance may change these terms. The version on `main` is the one in force.
