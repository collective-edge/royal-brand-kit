Direction · is this good news

Color follows favorable against unfavorable. It never follows the arithmetic sign of the delta and never follows the direction of the arrow. Every one of these carries an arrow or icon and a word, so color is never the only thing holding the meaning.

Diverging · polarity

Two hues away from a neutral middle, for distance above or below a baseline. The poles are red and teal, not red and green: red against green collapses into one color under red-green color blindness.
